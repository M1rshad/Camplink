OPERATIONAL NOTES (from operator's booking sheet — relevant for guest briefing)
- Hot water in toilets is LPG-limited to 1 hour each morning only, due to gas shortage
- Card/UPI payment can be unreliable due to poor network — advise guests to carry cash
- Approach road from Vattavada bus stand (1.5 km) is rough — advise driving slowly
- Mobile network: only BSNL and Jio work reliably; WiFi is available on-site
- Loudspeakers/music restricted after 10 PM per local police instruction
- Drug use strictly prohibited, will be reported to authorities
- Booking is confirmed only on receipt of advance payment
- Guest documentation required at check-in: name, address, phone, ID proof (Aadhaar),
  check-in/out dates, adult/child count, room count, total rent, advance paid

ADDITIONAL GUEST NOTES (from operator, Saj)
- Only pre-ordered/pre-booked food from their menu is available at the restaurant —
  guests cannot walk in and order freely
- Pambadumshola National Park forest check post sits on the route in; normal entry
  hours are 6 AM to 5 PM, so guests arriving/transiting outside that window need to
  plan around it
- Koviloor–Chilanthiyaar stretch (1 km) is a rough/gutter road — flag for guests
  driving themselves

NEARBY ATTRACTIONS (per operator, distances from the resort)
- Top Station viewpoint — 12 km / ~35 min, on the way to the resort. Sunrise, mist,
  and valley views.
- Pambadumshola National Park — 9 km / ~30 min, on the way. Trekking bookable via
  Kerala Forest Department; shola forest views, also passes grandis forest.
- Chilanthiyar Waterfalls — 4 km / ~15 min. Scenic view of the waterfall's start;
  reached via an off-road jeep ride.
- Strawberry & winter vegetable farms, Pazhathottam viewpoint — nearest, ~5 km.
