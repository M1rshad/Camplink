Mammal Valley Inn, Vattavada — Package Details

LOCATION
Tucked amidst vegetable gardens, in a valley, 250 meters walkable distance from the main road.
Location: 1.5 km from Koviloor (Land of Temples) bus station, towards Chilanthiyar Road.
Only 4x4 jeeps can access the last 250 meters — visitors park nearby and take a short scenic walk in.

ABOUT VATTAVADA (from brochure)
Known as the "Vegetable Village of Kerala" — a remote, peaceful village surrounded by lush
farmlands, home to a mostly Tamil-speaking farming community. Limited modern facilities,
mostly off-road routes — best explored by jeep or bike.
